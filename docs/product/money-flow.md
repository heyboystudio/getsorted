# Money flow

> Not tax or legal advice. Confirm VAT treatment, invoicing-on-behalf and payment-provider rules with an accountant and the chosen provider before launch.

## Principles

1. **Integer cents, ZAR only.** Columns named `*_cents` (bigint). Use a Money value object (e.g. `brick/money`); never floats, never `round()` on money.
2. **Sortd never holds customer money in its own bank account.** The payment provider collects, holds (where supported) and splits. Sortd instructs releases.
3. **Webhooks are the source of truth** for payment status, not the browser redirect.
4. **Every money movement is a ledger entry.** Balances are derived from `ledger_entries`, never stored as a single mutable number.
5. **Idempotency everywhere**: each provider event and each payout instruction has a unique key; processing the same event twice changes nothing.

## Who pays what

| Item | Paid by | Paid to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quote labour | Customer | Pro (minus commission) | Commission target 10–12% of labour (exact rate is an open question) |
| Quote materials | Customer | Pro | No commission on materials (to confirm) |
| Call-out fee | Customer | Pro | Treated as labour for commission |
| Booking / service fee | Customer | Sortd | Optional, open question; default R0 |
| Commission | Pro (deducted) | Sortd | Sortd issues the pro a commission invoice/statement |

## Flow

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Customer
    participant S as Sortd
    participant P as Payment provider
    participant Pro as Pro
    C->>S: Accept quote
    S->>P: Create deposit payment (if deposit > 0)
    C->>P: Pay deposit (card / instant EFT / wallet)
    P-->>S: Webhook: deposit succeeded (signed)
    S->>S: Ledger: customer funds held
    Pro->>S: Start work (in_progress)
    S->>P: Release deposit to Pro (minus commission share)
    Pro->>S: Mark complete + final invoice
    C->>P: Pay final invoice
    P-->>S: Webhook: final succeeded
    S->>S: Job completed; schedule payout next business day
    S->>P: Payout to Pro (minus commission)
    P-->>S: Webhook: payout paid
```

## Agreed final amount (spec 018)

- `service_jobs.agreed_final_cents` starts as the accepted estimate's total. It changes only through a final-amount proposal: lowering applies at once, and raising needs the customer's approval.
- The final invoice is for the agreed final amount minus the deposit already paid. If the deposit is more than the agreed amount, the difference is refunded.
- If the price isn't agreed, the pro may cancel before work starts, and the customer gets any deposit back in full.

## Deposit rules

- Pro chooses a deposit of 0–50% of the quote total when quoting (cap configurable).
- Deposit is released to the pro when they mark the job `in_progress` (default), so they can buy materials. **Open question:** release earlier for materials-heavy jobs?
- If the job is cancelled before `in_progress`, the deposit is refunded in full unless the cancellation rules below say otherwise.

## Cancellation and refund defaults (configurable, confirm with legal)

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Customer cancels before accepting a quote | Free |
| Customer cancels after accepting, before deposit | Free |
| Customer cancels > 48 h before scheduled date, deposit paid | Full deposit refund |
| Customer cancels < 48 h before scheduled date | Pro keeps the call-out fee (if quoted); rest refunded |
| Pro cancels after acceptance | Full refund to customer; pro gets a reliability strike |
| Dispute resolved for customer | Full or partial refund, recorded by admin |
| Dispute resolved for pro | Funds released |
| Chargeback after payout | Recovered from the pro's future payouts (pro agreement must allow this) |

## Ledger (conceptual)

`ledger_entries`: `id, service_job_id, account, direction (debit|credit), amount_cents, currency, source_type, source_id, idempotency_key, occurred_at`.

Accounts: `customer_funds_held`, `pro_payable`, `platform_commission`, `platform_fees`, `refunds`, `provider_fees`.

Rule: for every event, debits = credits. A nightly job reconciles ledger totals against the provider's settlement report and alerts on any difference.

## VAT and documents (to confirm with an accountant)

- Quotes and invoices are issued **by the pro**, generated by Sortd on their behalf, showing the pro's details and VAT number if VAT-registered.
- Sortd issues a commission statement to each pro per payout.
- Sortd registers for VAT once compulsory (turnover threshold), or earlier if advised.
- Every document is an immutable PDF stored privately, with a sequential number per issuer.

## Payment provider requirements (for selection later)

Must have: marketplace split or hold-and-release, payouts to any SA bank account next business day, instant EFT, card with 3-D Secure, Apple Pay / Google Pay, hosted checkout (keeps Sortd out of card-data scope), refunds and partial refunds via API, signed webhooks, sandbox, ZAR settlement.

Shortlist to evaluate: Paystack, Peach Payments, Ozow, PayFast. Integrate behind `App\Contracts\PaymentGateway` so the choice can change.
