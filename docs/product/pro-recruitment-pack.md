# Pro recruitment pack

For the founder and anyone helping recruit. Goal (roadmap, Phase L): **5 to 10 approved pros per trade (plumbing, electrical, painting, tiling) in 2 or 3 launch suburbs** before `GETSORTED_REQUIRE_PROS` is switched on. Launch suburbs: Berea / central (Morningside, Musgrave, Berea, Glenwood) and the North (Durban North, Umhlanga, La Lucia) first; see `docs/product/launch-area.md`.

Use the tracker `docs/product/pro-recruitment-tracker.csv` (open it in Excel or Google Sheets).

## The pitch in one breath
"GetSorted sends you real jobs from households near you, with the problem already described and photos, so you quote without wasting a trip. **It costs nothing to join and nothing to quote.** You only pay when a client picks you to come and look, and for now even that is free. You deal with the client directly and get paid by them directly."

## What a pro gets (and what they do not)
- Jobs matched by trade and distance from their base address (their own work radius).
- Up to 10 pros see each job and up to 5 estimates are accepted, so everyone has a fair chance and clients are not flooded.
- The client's name, phone and address only after they choose the pro.
- A verified badge when their registration is checked (PIRB for plumbing, registered electrician for electrical).
- Reviews that belong to the pro and build a reputation.
- **No commission on the job and no payment through GetSorted.** At launch the introduction fee is off. Later it is R99 per introduction, the first 10 free, with at least 30 days' notice (see the Pro agreement).
- Not offered: guaranteed work, exclusivity, or help collecting payment.

## Who we want
- Sole traders and small teams who answer their phone, show up, and give a price they stand behind.
- Based in or near the launch suburbs, with a work radius of 10 to 15 km.
- Plumbers (PIRB-registered preferred), electricians (registered, for compliance certificates), painters, tilers.
- Not a gate: registration is a badge, not a condition, except where the law requires it for the work (electrical certificate of compliance).

## What they need to apply (about 15 minutes on their phone)
1. A South African mobile and an email address (or a Google account).
2. A photo of their ID document and a proof of address (utility bill or bank statement).
3. A clear profile photo of themselves.
4. If they have one: PIRB card or electrician registration (and the number).
5. Two references (name, mobile, how they know the pro): we phone them.
6. A short line about the business, their trades and where they work.
7. VAT number only if registered.

## Onboarding steps to give them
1. Go to **usesorted.co.za/pros/join**, tap Apply, create an account (email or Google).
2. Confirm your email, add your mobile number.
3. Fill in the application: business, trades, work area, documents, references, a short bio.
4. Submit. We review within **2 business days**. We may ask for a clearer document or a change.
5. Once approved, install the site as an app (Add to Home Screen) and **turn on notifications**. New jobs reach you by notification and email.
6. When a job arrives: open it, tap **Accept job**, ask the client questions in chat, then **Send an estimate** (a price or a range, plus any call-out fee). Keep contact details out of chat until the client chooses you.
7. When the client chooses you, you see their name, phone and address. Visit, give the final quote, do the work, get paid directly. Tap **Mark as done** and ask for a review.

## Where to find pros
- Existing contacts and referrals: ask each approved pro for two more.
- Local trade supply counters (plumbing and electrical wholesalers) and hardware stores: leave a card.
- Facebook groups for Durban tradespeople and "Durban handyman" or "plumbers in Durban" groups (read each group's rules before posting).
- PIRB and ECSA/ESASA-registered member lists (publicly searchable; contact, do not scrape).
- Suburb community groups and WhatsApp groups where residents ask for recommendations (ask the admin first, do not spam).
- Apprentices and ex-employees of larger firms who now work on their own.

## Messages to copy
**WhatsApp / SMS (short):** "Hi [name], I'm [you] from GetSorted in Durban. We send plumbing/electrical/painting/tiling jobs from households near you, already described with photos. Free to join, no fee to quote. 15 minutes to apply: usesorted.co.za/pros/join. Happy to walk you through it. Can I call you?"

**Email subject:** "Free jobs from Durban households, no joining fee". Body: the pitch above, the application list, and your phone number.

**Call script:** (1) Who you are and why you called. (2) Ask what trade and which suburbs they cover. (3) Ask how they get work now and what annoys them (wasted call-outs, tyre-kickers, no-shows). (4) Explain what GetSorted does about that. (5) Ask for a yes: "Can I send you the link now and stay on the line while you apply?" (6) Book a follow-up in 2 days.

## Common questions
- **"What does it cost?"** Joining and quoting are free. The introduction fee is off at launch; later R99 per introduction, first 10 free, with 30 days' notice.
- **"Who pays me?"** The client, directly, for the work. GetSorted does not hold money.
- **"What if I have no PIRB?"** You can still join. You will not have the badge; some clients prefer registered pros.
- **"Do you share my number?"** Only with a client who has chosen you, and only that client.
- **"Will you send me jobs outside my area?"** No. You set your radius and can pause any time.
- **"Can I leave?"** Yes, any time. We refund unused credit if the fee is on.

## How we know recruiting is working
Weekly: pros contacted, applied, submitted, approved, active (accepted at least one job), and estimates sent per pro. Target before launch: **5 approved per trade in each of the 2 launch areas** (a third area optional). The admin dashboard (spec 027) shows stalled jobs and the success measures once real jobs arrive.
